Trades Clash

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 10, 2026  ·  Last updated: August 10, 2026

Trades Clash ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a PvP trading simulation game that uses historical market data and virtual, in-game currency for entertainment purposes only. Trades Clash does not involve real trading, real securities, or real financial transactions of any kind — all trading activity inside the App is simulated and has no monetary value outside the App.

This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use Trades Clash, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

Trades Clash is developed and operated by an independent developer ("Matan"), based in Israel. For any privacy-related question, see the Contact Us section below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum amount of data needed to run the App's account system, gameplay, and purchases.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, health data, or browsing history, and we do not use the App to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and authenticate you via Sign in with Apple.
  • To operate core gameplay: matching you with other players, tracking virtual currency, trade outcomes, and leaderboards.
  • To process in-app purchases of virtual currency and maintain accurate purchase records.
  • To maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the App (e.g., diagnosing crashes and bugs).
  • To communicate with you about your account or respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. How Your Information Is Stored & Shared

Your account and gameplay data is stored using Supabase, a third-party backend/database provider, which hosts our servers and enforces access controls on our behalf. Supabase acts as our data processor and does not use your data for its own purposes. You can review Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

We also share limited data with Apple as required to operate Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases, governed by Apple's own privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy.

We do not share your personal data with any other third party, except where required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the App.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and gameplay data for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we are required to retain certain records (e.g., purchase records) for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposes.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access & correction: you can review and update your in-game profile information from within the App.
  • Deletion: you may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us (see below).
  • Sign in with Apple controls: you can manage or revoke the App's access to your Apple ID at any time in your device's Settings > [your name] > Sign in & Security > Apps Using Apple ID.
  • Depending on where you live (e.g., EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights, such as the right to data portability or to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

Trades Clash is rated 13+ on the App Store and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (including the security controls provided by Apple and Supabase) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Users

The App is operated from Israel. If you use the App from outside Israel, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in Israel or other countries where our service providers (such as Apple and Supabase) operate.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable law. We will update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
